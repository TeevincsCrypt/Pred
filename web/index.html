Stay ahead of the open, not behind it

PRED shows every Ghost Event in one place: the leading hypothesis, how confident the model is, and whether reality agreed. Liquidity noise gets filtered out, real catalysts get confirmed, and wrong calls stay on the record.

Six focused agents, each with one job. Every probability can be traced to the evidence behind it, and PRED says so when it doesn't know.
