**0:00 – Hook.** "Tokenized stocks trade 24/7. The U.S. market doesn't. When NVIDIA's token moves at 5pm on a Sunday, who explains it? PRED does. PRED finds what the market knows before the news says it."

**0:15 – Framing.** Open the dashboard in Demo mode and point at the SIMULATED SCENARIO label. "This is a deterministic replay, so you can see the full lifecycle in three minutes. Everything simulated is labeled simulated. LIVE mode runs the same agents on Bitget market data."

**0:25 – Steps 1–3.** Click *Start demo*, then *Next* twice. Point to the market-status pill: US MARKET CLOSED · WEEKEND. The bars stream in, then NVDAx jumps. "The Detector compares every 5-minute window with a 2-hour baseline: price z-score, volume, volatility, spread, and how much of the move peers explain."

**0:45 – Steps 4–6.** GHOST EVENT #184: +2.34%, volume +640%, catalyst unknown. Run the investigation. "Ten channels. Every source reports a status. 'Nothing found' is evidence too, and a missing connector is shown as missing." Show the Catalyst Graph building itself.

**1:15 – Steps 7–8.** Hypotheses: Company-specific 67%. Open the evidence list and show the weights. "These are model confidence estimates, capped short of certainty. Every edge in the graph is a weight you can inspect." Point to the action posture: WAIT.

**1:35 – Steps 9–11.** Fast-forward overnight. Revisions 2–4 are appended as the move holds and discussion accelerates. Use the rev ‹ › buttons: "PRED never overwrites its reasoning." Reveal the simulated release, then show CATALYST CONFIRMED with the original hypothesis, the confirming evidence, and 13h 50m from detection to confirmation.

**2:05 – Steps 12–13.** Reaction: +1.66% → +3.87%, estimate +2.36%, from 26 comparables. Run the Monday session. Actual reaction +3.44%: direction ✓, range ✓.

**2:30 – Steps 14–15.** PRED Memory: 184 events. "The other 183 are a simulated backtest run through the same models. Look at the failures: wrong catalysts, liquidity anomalies, unresolved events. The calibration chart shows where PRED is over- or under-confident."

**2:45 – Close.** Point to CONSIDER TRADE and `/api/signals`. "PRED does not trade. It publishes verified signals with a risk policy for a separate execution agent, such as one on Bitget Agent Hub. The product is the loop: Detect, Investigate, Verify, Learn. PRED: 24/7 market event intelligence for tokenized equities."
