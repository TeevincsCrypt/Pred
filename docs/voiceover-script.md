# PRED voice-over: silent walkthrough (2:48)

**Word count:** about 390 words, which is roughly 2 min 40 s of speech.

## Recording tips

- In Clipchamp, drop the video on the timeline, then choose **Record & create → Audio** (or record on your phone and import the file). Record one row at a time, and drag each clip to its start time.
- The 2:26 sentence is the longest (35 words, about 14 s). Start it the moment the trade panel lights up. It can run a second or two into the closing shot.
- If a line runs long, cut words rather than speed up. Only these must stay word for word: "simulated" at 0:21, "simulated here" at 1:33, "in this demo that history is simulated" at 2:13, and the execution sentence at 2:26.
- Add quiet background music at 10–15% volume if you like. Keep the voice clearly on top.
