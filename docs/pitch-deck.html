Our insight
03

A Ghost Event is a move the market
made before anyone explained it.

PRED opens one only when all three conditions hold. Anything else is logged with the reason and ignored.

1

Abnormal activity

A statistically large move (|z| ≥ 3.5) confirmed by volume, on a liquid baseline, not a single trade on an empty book.

2

Wall Street closed

NYSE calendar: nights, weekends, holidays and early closes.

3

Tokenized market live

The Bitget instrument is online and actively trading, with fresh 1-minute data.

How it works
04

Six agents, one lifecycle.

DETECTLive Bitget 1-min bars vs a 2-hour baseline
INVESTIGATESEC filings, news, peers, sibling tokens, BTC/ETH, market breadth
HYPOTHESIZE6 competing catalysts, evidence-weighted probabilities
VERIFYOfficial evidence or price behaviour, deadline at the U.S. open
PREDICTReaction range to the U.S. close from comparable events
LEARNScores direction, range and catalyst on every outcome

Transparent, not a black box

Every probability is prior + evidence weights → softmax, capped short of certainty, with a "why N%" breakdown and every revision kept.

LLM as narrator, never as judge

Claude or a Groq-hosted model writes the explanation from PRED's cited evidence only. It never sets numbers, confirms events or trades.

Why trust it

Honest by construction.

  • Confirmation needs proof: an official release or a material SEC filing.
  • No false certainty: probabilities are capped and labelled as model estimates.

From intelligence to action
08

Human-approved execution on Bitget.
Agents never trade.

Trade plan → review → approve → order

PRED drafts a sized plan from a Ghost Event: side, quantity at Bitget's precision, stop, target, thesis and evidence. The operator reviews it and must press Approve & execute with the exact confirmation phrase.

Safety built in

The server rebuilds and rechecks every order against fresh prices, balance and per-order, per-position and daily limits. One order per plan, even on double clicks. A full audit trail. Disabled by default.

Bitget Unified Trading API v3 (/api/v3/trade/place-order). Verified against a simulated exchange in the test suite and with read-only checks on the live account API.
