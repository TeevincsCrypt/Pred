Abstract

Tokenized U.S. equities now trade around the clock on Bitget, while the underlying U.S. market is open for 6.5 hours a day. In the remaining 17.5 hours, and all weekend, prices can react to information before any headline, filing or analyst note explains it. We call such a move a Ghost Event. PRED is a live system that watches Bitget's tokenized-equity markets continuously and opens a Ghost Event only when three conditions hold at once. It then runs an evidence pipeline over public sources (SEC EDGAR filings, news and related-asset behaviour), maintains a transparent probability model over six competing catalyst categories, and resolves each event against authoritative evidence or observed price behaviour. It estimates the reaction into the next U.S. close from comparable past events, and scores itself on every outcome. PRED never fabricates data, never presents a model estimate as a certainty, and never trades on its own. An optional execution layer lets a human act on PRED's intelligence through Bitget, with explicit approval for every order.

01 The problem: prices move before the news

A U.S. stock that trades only in the regular session cannot react to overnight information until 9:30 a.m. ET. Its tokenized counterpart can react at 2 a.m. That creates a new, observable phenomenon: a tokenized equity moves abnormally while its home market is closed, and for minutes or hours there is no public explanation.

  • Traders see a price move with no context. Is it information, a sector rotation, a crypto-driven flow, or a thin order book?
  • Existing tools either alert on price alone (noisy, no explanation) or summarise news after the fact (too late, and blind to moves that precede news).
  • LLM "market analysts" produce fluent explanations that are often unsourced or invented. That is a liability in finance.

PRED targets the gap between the move and the explanation. It doesn't try to predict prices from nothing. It finds moves that already happened while Wall Street slept, works out what could explain them, and says honestly how sure it is.

02 The Ghost Event

A Ghost Event is opened only when all three conditions hold. An abnormal move that fails any of them is logged as "not a Ghost Event" with the reason, and nothing is opened.

1 · Abnormal activity
A price move that is statistically large for this instrument, confirmed by volume, on a liquid baseline.
2 · Traditional market closed
Per the NYSE calendar: nights, weekends, holidays and 13:00 early closes (2025–2027).
3 · Tokenized market live
The Bitget instrument is online, candles are fresh, trades occurred in the last 15 minutes, and it isn't a thin market.

05 Detection

For each instrument, PRED compares the last 5 one-minute bars against a 120-bar baseline:

σ = std(1-min log returns over the baseline) z = ln(P_now / P_anchor) / (σ · √5) volume ratio = mean(window volume) / median(baseline volume) (capped at 50×) score = 0.9·max(0,|z|−1) + 1.2·log2(volume ratio) + 0.5·(volatility ratio−1) + 0.3·(spread ratio−1) abnormal ⇔ |move| ≥ 0.6% and ( (|z| ≥ 3.5 and volume ≥ 2.5×) or score ≥ 6 )

These guards come from production experience:

  • Contiguity: a data gap (a restart or a halt) is never read as a price move.
  • Liquid baseline: at least 60% of baseline minutes must have traded. On thin pre-market books a median volume of zero once made a single trade look like a 10¹⁰% volume spike. That case is now suppressed with a logged reason.
  • Thin markets: instruments below a minimum 24h turnover are excluded.
  • Cooldown: one open event per instrument until its outcome is measured, so a single catalyst isn't counted twice.

06 Hypotheses: a transparent model

Every Ghost Event carries a probability distribution over six catalyst categories:

CategoryPrior (log-odds)Typical supporting evidence
Company-specific catalyst0Move not explained by peers; filings; company-named headlines
Sector-wide repricing0Related companies moving together
Macro / market-wide flow−0.5Broad tokenized-equity breadth; BTC/ETH moves
Liquidity anomaly−0.2Wide spreads, reversal, no news
Scheduled event pre-positioning−1.5A calendar entry close to the move
Unknown / unexplained+0.3Absence of evidence
score_k = prior_k + Σ weight(evidence → k) (caps: news ≤ 2.4, filings ≤ 2.0) p_k = softmax(score_k / 1.5) (≤ 0.90; ≤ 0.95 if authoritative)

Each hypothesis shows the evidence for and against it, with weights and sources, the number of independent sources, its change since the previous revision, and a "why N%" breakdown. Revisions are append-only, so earlier reasoning is never overwritten. The numbers are model confidence estimates. PRED says so wherever they appear, and calibrates them against outcomes in PRED Memory.

07 Verification and resolution

PRED never forces a confirmation. An event resolves in one of three ways:

  • CONFIRMED needs authoritative evidence: an official company release, or a material SEC filing tied to the move.
  • INVALIDATED means the evidence contradicts the leading hypothesis. For example, the move fully reverted and the model now leads with liquidity.
  • At the U.S. open plus 30 minutes, PRED closes verification, because the question a Ghost Event asks is whether the move can be explained before Wall Street trades it:
    • If the move faded (20% or less of it left) with no official news, it was liquidity, not information: INVALIDATED, or CONFIRMED as liquidity if PRED already led with that.
    • If it held, the event is UNRESOLVED · held: the market kept a move nobody has explained yet.

08 Reaction prediction

When a catalyst is confirmed, or the leading hypothesis reaches 70%, the Reaction agent estimates the move from the prediction time to the first U.S. regular-session close. It finds comparable past events in PRED Memory (same direction, similar size and volume profile, same catalyst category), weights them by similarity, and reports the weighted 20th/50th/80th percentiles as a range with a confidence score. If fewer than three comparable events with known outcomes exist, it reports "Insufficient live history" instead of producing a number. Live memory starts at zero, so early reaction estimates are deliberately absent or labelled LOW CONFIDENCE.

09 Learning: PRED Memory

At the U.S. close after each event, PRED measures the actual reaction and scores itself:

  • whether it got the direction right;
  • whether the move landed inside the predicted range;
  • whether the leading catalyst matched the verified one.

It also tracks how well its stated confidence matches reality (a reliability curve and a Brier score) and tags each failure mode. These records feed the next reaction estimates. The track record is live-only: nothing synthetic is ever mixed into it. The separately labelled demo uses its own simulated memory.

10 The AI analyst (optional)

A language model (Claude, or a Groq-hosted open model on the free tier) can write the plain-English explanation of the leading hypothesis. It sees only the evidence PRED collected, must cite evidence IDs, and is instructed never to invent facts or numbers. It never sets prices, probabilities, confirmations or trade decisions. Those come from the transparent model above. If the model is unavailable or rate-limited, PRED uses a deterministic template, so the system never depends on an LLM to function.

11 Human-approved execution

PRED is an intelligence layer, not a trading bot. For an operator who wants to act on a Ghost Event, PRED includes an execution layer on Bitget's Unified Trading API (POST /api/v3/trade/place-order). It is built so that no agent, background job, stream or webhook can place an order:

  • Separate module. The trading code is isolated from the agents, and a test fails the build if any agent imports it. There is exactly one call site that submits an order.
  • Explicit approval. Every order needs a logged-in operator, a CSRF-protected POST, and the exact confirmation phrase (e.g. BUY 0.12 NVDAUSDT).
  • Server-built orders. The browser names a plan ID only; the server rebuilds the order from the stored plan and fresh Bitget data.
  • Revalidation before sending. Instrument status and precision, price drift, balance, account mode, and the per-order, per-position and daily notional limits are all rechecked.
  • One order per plan. An atomic database claim makes concurrent approvals produce exactly one order. A timeout is reconciled by client order ID, never resent.
  • Accurate status. SUBMITTED means Bitget accepted the order; FILLED comes only from Bitget's order status. Every step is recorded in an audit trail.

Status. Human-approved execution through Bitget’s API is built and tested against a simulated Bitget, and disabled by default. Every order needs explicit human approval, fresh price and balance checks, and cannot be placed twice. Agents cannot trade.

12 Integrity principles

No synthetic data in live mode. Missing fields are null and shown as n/a. The simulated demo is isolated and labelled SIMULATED everywhere.
Provenance on everything. Every evidence item carries its class (OBSERVED / SCHEDULED / HISTORICAL), source and timestamp. URLs are validated before display.
Honest status. Every source reports CONNECTED, DEGRADED or DISCONNECTED with the actual reason. "No active Ghost Events" is a valid state.
No false certainty. Probabilities are capped short of 100% and labelled as model estimates. Reaction estimates require history.

13 Limitations

  • Evidence weights are hand-set. Live calibration exists precisely to show where they are wrong.
  • Headline sentiment is not inferred. A confirmed catalyst establishes that something happened, not which way it cuts.
  • Free news sources are rate-limited and can block shared cloud IPs. PRED degrades gracefully and says so, but coverage depends on them.
  • The reaction model needs live history. Early predictions are rare and labelled low-confidence by design.
  • Execution is single-operator. A multi-user product would need per-user key custody, limits and regulatory review.

14 Roadmap

  • Automatic earnings and corporate-event calendar, so SCHEDULED hypotheses need no manual input.
  • Additional authoritative feeds: company investor-relations RSS and exchange notices.
  • Learned evidence weights once live memory reaches statistical size.
  • Alerts (webhook, Telegram) when a Ghost Event opens or resolves.
  • Bitget Agent Hub integration: publish PRED's verified signals to external, risk-controlled agents.

PRED provides market intelligence, not investment advice. Probabilities are model estimates. Tokenized equities and derivatives carry significant risk.
